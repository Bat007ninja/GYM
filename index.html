My Programme

Same block structure every session — Block A: choose 1 of 2. Block B: choose 2–3 of 3. Block C: choose 2–3 of 3 — with different exercises each day, Monday–Saturday. Tick off what you did and log your weight/reps each time — it builds a dated history per exercise, saved on this device.

All 6 days are the real programme from Top Condition PT. It's expected to fully refresh roughly every 3 months — when it does, update js/programme-data.js. Your logged history lives only in this browser — use "Backup" to save a copy you can restore on another device, or "Export to Excel" to view/analyse it.
